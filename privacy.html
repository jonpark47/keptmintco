Ginamon Privacy Policy

Last updated September 18, 2026

Ginamon is a private tool used by two eBay sellers to keep their own sales records in one place. It is not a public service and has no customer accounts.

What it accesses

Only after an eBay seller explicitly authorizes it through eBay's own sign-in and consent screen, Ginamon reads that seller's order information with read-only permission:

  • Order numbers, dates, and status
  • Item titles, quantities, sale amounts, and eBay fees
  • The buyer's eBay username, used only as an order reference

It does not change, create, or delete anything on the seller's eBay account, and it cannot list items, message buyers, or move money.

How it is used

The order information is used solely to record sales and profit in the sellers' own private ledger. It is not sold, shared, published, or used for advertising or profiling.

Where it is stored

Order records are kept in a private database that only the two sellers can reach. The authorization token that lets Ginamon read orders is kept in a restricted file on a private server and is never shown in the tool or shared with anyone.

Retention and removal

Records are kept for as long as the sellers need them for their own bookkeeping. A seller can revoke access at any time in their eBay account under permissions for connected apps, and Ginamon will immediately be unable to read anything further. If a buyer's data must be removed, or eBay notifies us that an eBay account was deleted, the related records are deleted.

Contact

Questions or removal requests can be sent through eBay messaging to the seller account that authorized this connection.
